## 🎯 About the Project

**Living Statues: Iconography of Sports** merges physical education pedagogy with European art history and biomechanical posture analysis:
1. **Sculptural Iconography:** Analyzing historical European sports sculptures (e.g., *Discobolus of Myron*, *Boxer at Rest*, *Borghese Gladiator*) to understand ancient physical culture and aesthetic ideals.
2. **Functional Anatomy:** Examining muscle groups (agonist/antagonist pairs), postural stability, center of gravity, and joint kinematics in physical education.
3. **Tableau Vivant (Living Tableaux):** Students recreate classical sculptural poses live, utilizing core strength, controlled breathing, and theatrical poise, documented through dual-layer digital visual comparisons.

## 🗓️ 4-Month Project Implementation Plan

* **Month 1: Orientation & Sculptural Iconography**
  * Student & teacher onboarding on TwinSpace and Padlet boards.
  * Research into antique athletic sculptures and Olympic ideals.
  * Transnational mixed student teams formation.
* **Month 2: Functional Anatomy & Postural Labs**
  * Posture analysis, joint angles, and agonist/antagonist muscle mapping in P.E.
  * Digital anatomy overlays and ergonomic adolescent health debates.
* **Month 3: Living Tableaux & Digital Media Production**
  * Tableau Vivant student live re-enactments.
  * Dual-layer visual compositions (statue vs. live performance).
  * Interactive audio and anatomical tagging via Web 2.0 tools.
* **Month 4: Common Products & Final Dissemination**
  * Common Product 1: Interactive Anatomy & Sports Calendar (E-book / Poster).
  * Common Product 2: 3D Virtual Gallery Exhibition (*Artsteps / Spatial.io*).
  * Online student evaluation webinar and Quality Label dossier compilation.

## 🛡️ eSafety & Ethics Policy

* **Parental Consent & GDPR:** Verified parental permission forms are archived prior to any photo or video capture.
* **Child Privacy:** No student full surnames or private social media links are published publicly.
* **Copyright & Netiquette:** Sculpture images and reference media strictly observe Creative Commons licenses and academic citations.
