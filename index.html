Living Statues: Iconography of Sports

From Antiquity to Modern Era

Welcome to Our Project

Exploring the historical bridge between classical art, sculpture iconography, and modern athletic disciplines through student-led creative performances and digital tools.

Project Goals

To integrate sports history with digital storytelling, foster intercultural collaboration among European schools, and promote active citizenship and arts.

About the Project

Discover the core concepts and educational objectives behind our work.

Project Concept

Students examine how sports and physical culture have been depicted through centuries in statues, pottery, and paintings. By recreating these poses as living statues, they connect historical iconography with physical education.
