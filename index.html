About the Project

Interdisciplinary integration: Physical Education, Arts, Anatomy & Digital Tech

🏺 Sculptural Iconography

Investigating classical sports sculptures, aesthetic ideals, and the representation of athletic motion across European history.

⚡ Functional Anatomy

Analyzing agonist/antagonist muscle groups, joint angles, center of gravity, and postural balance in physical education.

🎭 Living Tableaux (Tableau Vivant)

Students recreate historical statues with precise body posture, dual-layer visuals, and interactive multimedia overlays.

★ OFFICIAL eTWINNING PROJECT • 4-MONTH ROADMAP ★

Detailed Roadmap & Activity Plan

Step-by-step implementation from sculptural research to virtual museum

Month 1

Orientation & Sculptural Iconography

  • Student & partner onboarding on TwinSpace / Padlet.
  • Formation of transnational mixed student teams.
  • Research on historical athletic sculptures (Discobolus, Borghese Gladiator, Olympic art).
  • Publishing art history & aesthetic ideals cards.
Month 2

Functional Anatomy & Postural Labs

  • Mapping agonist/antagonist muscle groups and joint angles.
  • Designing digital anatomical overlays via graphic apps.
  • Center of gravity & postural balance practical tests in P.E. classes.
  • Ergonomic and adolescent postural health debates.
Month 3

Living Tableaux & Digital Media

  • Tableau Vivant student re-enactments with precise posture & lighting.
  • Dual-layer visual composition (historic statue + live recreation).
  • Interactive audio & anatomical tagging using multimedia tools (Genially/ThingLink).
Month 4

Common Products & Dissemination

  • Product 1: Interactive Anatomy & Sports Calendar (E-book & printable poster).
  • Product 2: 3D Virtual Gallery Exhibition (Artsteps / Spatial.io).
  • Online final student webinar & project evaluation surveys.
  • Quality Label documentation compilation.
